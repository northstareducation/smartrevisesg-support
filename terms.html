Terms of use

Updated 19 September 2026

SGSmartRevise provides free primary-level practice, standard explanations, learning milestones and local Parent Learning Reports. The current app has no in-app purchases, subscriptions or AI chat.

Apple’s standard licence

The App Store listing uses Apple’s Standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement. These learning and service notes supplement that agreement and do not replace it.

Learning support

The app supports practice alongside teaching and help from a trusted adult. Questions, hints and explanations should be discussed when something is uncertain. The app does not guarantee an exam result or replace curriculum assessment.

Reports and recommendations

Reports use the activity recorded in this app. They cannot observe learning elsewhere. Readiness and difficulty recommendations are estimates, and comparative results depend on the number and type of questions attempted. They do not establish that the app caused a change in learning outcomes.

Garden stars and badges recognise practice milestones. They are not grades, intelligence measures or prizes with monetary value.

Family privacy and sharing

A nickname is enough to practise. A grown-up should protect the parent PIN and decide when to export or share a report. See the privacy policy for local storage, backups, PDF exports and older AWS account requests.

Availability and local records

Bundled practice works offline. Device storage, backups and operating-system settings affect the availability of local records. The app does not provide automatic account-based cloud synchronisation. Keep any exported reports in a place appropriate for the family’s privacy needs.

Respectful use and content

Use the app for learning and respect the privacy of other learners. Do not attempt to access another family’s information or bypass the parent report safeguard. Educational material and artwork remain subject to applicable copyright and licence terms.

Support

For support or requests about an older account, email help@northstareducation.io. Do not include passwords or parent PINs.
